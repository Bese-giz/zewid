## Publishing Checklist

- **Frankincense pack size:** The order lists 250 g while the product photo appears to show 100 g. Confirm the actual size before adding a weight to the retail title.
- **Retail units:** Verify the actual content of each supplier carton/set; “/3”, “/10”, “/20” and similar codes are wholesale descriptions, not retail unit counts.
- **Electrical goods:** Confirm plug type, rated voltage and power, controller inclusion, instructions and EU conformity documentation for the two Mogogo models and the hot plate.
- **Food:** Obtain the complete ingredients, allergens, nutrition declaration where required, best-before dates, origin information where required, and storage instructions. Provide mandatory food information in the languages required for sales in Finland.
- **Serving sets:** Verify exact material, number of parts, food-contact suitability, safe temperature range and dishwasher instructions. The term “food warmer” does not by itself establish an active heating function.
- **Cosmetics:** Check the complete INCI ingredient list, responsible person, required EU product notifications and Finnish/Swedish labelling before selling.
- **Rekebot furniture:** Confirm dimensions, materials, included accessories, assembly requirements and any LED/Bluetooth claims.
- **Stock and prices:** Set retail prices and mark products as available only after confirming incoming quantities.
