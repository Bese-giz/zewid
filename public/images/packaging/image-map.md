## Berbere – Ethiopian Spice Blend

Bring depth and aromatic spice to everyday cooking with Berbere. This finely ground blend can be stirred into wot, sauces, marinades and vegetable dishes. Add gradually and adjust the amount to suit your recipe.

## Mitmita – Ethiopian Chilli Spice Blend

Mitmita is an Ethiopian chilli seasoning with a bold, aromatic flavour. Use a small pinch as a finishing condiment on cooked meat, vegetables or cheese, or add it to sauces when you want extra heat. Start with a little and season to taste.

Alt: Zewid Market Mitmita 500 g in an opaque gold pouch with the new bilingual label and website QR code

## Bula – Ethiopian Enset Starch

Bula is the fine white starch extracted from enset, also known as false banana. It is used in Ethiopian cooking to prepare a smooth cooked porridge and can also be used to thicken soups. Mix with a little cool water before adding to hot liquid and cook while stirring.

Alt: Zewid Market Bula 1 kg in an opaque gold pouch with the new bilingual label and website QR code

## Korerima – Ethiopian Cardamom Powder

Korerima is Ethiopian cardamom, made from the seeds of Aframomum corrorima. Its warm, smoky and citrus-like aroma adds character to Ethiopian spice blends, stews and spiced butter. Add a small amount during cooking and adjust to taste.

## Ethiopian Mekelesha Spice Mix – 100 g

Mekelesha is commonly added near the end of cooking to give Ethiopian stews and sauces their finishing aroma. Use it in dishes such as doro wat and other richly seasoned recipes, adjusting the amount to taste.

## Ethiopian Sidamo Arabica Green Coffee Beans – 1 kg

These green Sidamo coffee beans are intended for customers who enjoy roasting coffee themselves. Roast the beans to your preferred level, then grind and brew them. They are particularly suitable for the Ethiopian coffee ceremony, where coffee is traditionally roasted fresh before serving.
